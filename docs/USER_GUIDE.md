# Using Little Room

Start with [setup](SETUP.md) if the app is not running yet. Keep the app tab and
local service open while using the timer, audible reminders and saving.

## Your first session

1. Choose **Countdown** or **Count up**. Open **Session settings** to choose
   focus and break lengths, completion behavior and break preferences.
2. Add optional tasks and tags beside the timer. You can edit them during focus.
3. Select an egg in the shop if you want that egg to study with you.
4. Start focus. The room enters a quiet view with a larger clock; navigation
   and the daily goal return when you pause or leave focus.
5. Use Pause when needed and the notes button to capture a thought without
   stopping work. Finish count-up with the checkmark. At a countdown target,
   choose a break or finish any overtime.

The daily goal counts completed sessions, rather than minutes. Change it in
Settings. Sessions shorter than one whole active minute receive no history,
reward or daily-goal credit.

## Timer controls and rules

| Choice or control | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Countdown + **Repeat alarm** | Reaching the target credits the session and repeats an alarm every eight seconds when Timer sound is enabled. Mute it or choose what to do next. |
| Countdown + **Ding + count up** | Reaching the target credits the session, then continues into overtime. Finish or start a break to settle its additional whole minutes. |
| Count up | Starts at zero. Pause/resume excludes paused time; Finish credits the whole active minutes. |
| Pomodoro pause | Two pauses, up to one minute each. A third pause or an overlong pause forfeits that session's Shilligems, including its overtime reward. Resume keeps remaining focus time; egg progress and Journal credit are retained. |
| Count-up or break pause | Unrestricted pauses. |
| Stop | Hold for five continuous seconds to abandon unfinished countdown or count-up work. Releasing early, Escape or losing focus cancels the hold. Space or Enter can also be held. Overtime uses Finish. |
| Short/long break | Starts when you choose it after reaching the focus target. The configured interval recommends a long break. |
| **Auto-start focus** | Optionally begins the next focus session when a running break ends. Loading an already expired break leaves the next session idle. |

Count-up and overtime tracking are capped at 24 hours. Rewards use whole active
minutes. Countdown receives its base credit at the target; overtime updates
that same session instead of adding another completion. While overtime is
ongoing, the gems credited to that session remain reserved until you finish or
start a break. Gems from earlier sessions remain available to spend.

Timer sound follows its own setting. If browser playback is blocked, a
completed Pomodoro offers **Enable alarm sound**. An open tab is needed for
audible reminders; the app does not provide a background system notification
service.

## Notes, tasks and the Journal

The floating Scratchpad and Journal Scratchpad edit the same saved notes.
The floating notes window stays open across navigation and focus changes until
you close it or toggle its notes button. Drag its move handle, or use the arrow
keys on the handle, to change its saved position.

On narrow screens, notes use a compact sheet with a visible **Minimize** action.
Other workspace controls are concealed while that sheet is open. Minimize it
to return to the timer or Journal.

Use the session checklist to track tasks and add tags such as Work or Study.
The Journal offers **Today**, **7 days** and **30 days**, following your local
calendar. It shows focus time, session count, average length, active days,
activity charts, tag totals and session history with tasks and tags. A session
with several tags contributes its full duration to each tag, so tag totals can
add up to more than the overall focus time.

## Eggs, pets and Shilligems

Focus earns **one Shilligem per whole minute**, plus any maxed-pet bonus. A basic
egg costs 25 Shilligems; a mystery roll costs 60. The shop's info button explains
the mystery rarity odds and its rare-or-better guarantee.

| Egg rarity | Focus needed |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | 25 minutes |
| Everyday | 50 minutes |
| Rare | 75 minutes |
| Dreamy | 100 minutes |

One selected egg studies with you per session. The oldest egg is selected by
default; select another or deselect it before starting. The session captures
its egg at the start, so changing the selection during work applies to the next
session. Credited progress stays with that egg and does not spill into another.
Hatch a ready egg from the shop.

The collection includes cats, dogs, squirrels, rabbits, foxes and deer in four
rarities. Undiscovered album tiles show rarity stars and can reveal the matching
egg. A duplicate of the same species and rarity upgrades the existing pet, up
to six upgrades and seven animations. Each maxed pet adds a 10% focus reward
bonus, capped at 50% total and rounded down. The bonus is captured when work
starts. Further copies of a maxed pet give five Shilligems.

Open a pet's details to choose its available animation and accessory.

## Make the room yours

Open **Room**, then **Arrange room** in the Decorate panel. Choose furniture or
a pet and drag it into place. Arrow keys nudge the selected item; Shift moves
it farther. The selection outline and X identify the item being edited.

Library tiles cycle through placed copies. Each furniture purchase adds one
copy; removing furniture returns that copy to the library. Release a drag to
save its position; press Escape during a drag to return to the last saved
position. For new furniture, **Cancel placement** keeps the copy in your
library; **Place & Done** confirms it and closes Arrange. The illustrated
background and furniture scale together.

The timer and **Daily goal** note have move handles and saved positions. They
stay within the room when its size changes. New rooms begin with a sofa and rug.

## Sound and motion

| Setting | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Timer sound | Completion chimes, repeat alarms and pause-limit alerts. |
| Interaction sounds and volume | Taps and other interface feedback. Preview sound plays a tap at the selected volume. |
| Ambient mixing | Procedural background sounds and their levels, independent of interaction volume. Timer alerts take priority in the mix. |
| Reduced motion | Quietens nonessential motion. The app also honors your operating system's reduced-motion preference. |

Sound settings are in Settings and Sound. A browser may require an interaction
before it allows audio playback.

## When saving needs attention

Open **Save details** to read the cause and next action. A service outage keeps
edits available in the current window; restart the service and choose
**Retry save**. A newer save from another window requires reviewing your edits
before loading the saved version. Recovery drafts are reference copies held in
memory until the window closes. Download and check the file if you need to keep
one. See [backup and recovery](SETUP.md#backup-and-recovery) for the full process.
